A implies B does not mean B implies A

October 10, 2026

People take a sentence that is true in one direction and use it backwards. That is a different sentence.

The mix up

The most important thing to understand is this. If A implies B, you only get one direction. You do not get the reverse.

People still use the reverse. They hear a rule, and the next time the result shows up they announce the cause.

Doctors do it. Lawyers do it. Engineers, journalists and managers do it.

So do people who never opened a logic book.

There is a name for the reversed sentence. It is called the converse. The jump from the result back to the cause is called affirming the consequent.

You can forget the names. The mistake is the same.

Think of this in the following way.

If it rains, the ground gets wet. Fine.

The ground is wet, so it rained. Not fine.

Someone may have washed a car. A pipe may be leaking. The gardener may have watered the plants. There may have been heavy dew.

Wet ground is what you can see. Rain is only one way to get it.

But there is another way to say the same thing.

A implies B tells you what follows from A. It does not tell you what must have come before B.

And trust me, once you can hear those two sentences as different, you will hear this mistake in almost every conversation you are already having.

Relationships

If he cared, he would remember the birthday. He remembered, so he cares.

A reminder on the phone can do that. So can a friend who texted him in the morning. So can guilt. So can a person who is good with dates and careless about everything else.

The memory is real. The feeling is a guess.

If someone is cheating, they pull away. She pulled away, so she is cheating.

Pulling away has a long list. A bad month at work. A parent in hospital. A fight with no words for it yet. Low mood. A dying phone. A person who goes quiet when they are only tired.

The cheating story is the one people are afraid of, so they pick it.

That is a harsh version of this mistake. The fear feels like proof. It is not proof.

If someone is interested, they make a plan. They made a plan, so they are interested.

People make plans out of politeness, out of loneliness, and because saying yes to Saturday is easier than saying no.

A plan shows that a plan was made. It does not measure interest.

If the relationship is healthy, you do not fight. You have not fought in months, so it is healthy.

Quiet can be peace. Quiet can also be two people who stopped bringing up the things that cause fights.

If they were serious, they would introduce you to their friends. They introduced you, so they are serious.

The dinner may already have been fixed. It may have been manners. It may have been someone who likes showing people the city.

You saw one evening. Seriousness is only one cause of that evening.

Health

If you have the flu, you get a fever. You have a fever, so you have the flu.

A fever has many causes. Some other infection. A reaction. Heat. A medicine.

In a house, the sentence that actually gets used is the second one. You are warm, so it is the flu, take this.

The first sentence never earned that.

If this tablet works, the pain goes down. The pain went down, so the tablet worked.

Pain also goes down because you lay still, because an hour passed, because you slept. The tablet may have done it. The clock does not tell you which.

This is how a glass of haldi doodh becomes the family cure. The headache left after the glass, so the glass fixed it.

The headache may already have been on its way out.

Remedies can help. Timing is still not a cause.

Doctors do this too.

And trust me, the degree on the wall does not remove the habit.

If it is bacterial, a marker they ordered tends to be high. The marker is high, so it is bacterial, so here are antibiotics.

Other things raise the same marker. A careful doctor treats the high number as a reason to look harder. A rushed OPD flips it.

The printout makes the flip look like science.

It is still a flip.

If you eat junk every day, you gain weight. They gained weight, so they must be eating junk.

Weight also moves with sleep, medicine, muscle, age, a thyroid, a year of stress. The junk food story is easy, and it sounds moral, so people use it.

You can watch an MBBS, a CA and a software engineer do this about a colleague between the lift and the desk.

If a person is fit, they can finish a short run. They finished the run, so they are fit.

Someone can drag themselves through a few kilometres, sleep four hours, and live on Maggi. The run happened. Fitness of the whole life was assumed.

If the treatment is working, the cough gets quieter. The cough got quieter, so the treatment is working.

Coughs also quiet down because the weather changed, because they skipped cigarettes for three days, because a virus finished its course. Parents like the second sentence. So do clinicians who want yesterday's decision to have been the right one.

Money

If a person is rich, they have a nice car. They have a nice car, so they are rich.

Cars get leased. Cars get gifted. Cars get bought on an EMI that will hurt for years.

The car is in the parking lot. The loan is not.

People still do this math out loud at weddings. A CA will do it about a neighbour and then go back to a spreadsheet.

Think of a package in the same way. If someone is genuinely strong, they can land an offer of ₹20,00,000 a year. They got ₹20,00,000, so they are genuinely strong.

The offer can be a hot year. It can be a college tag. It can be one round that went well. It can be a company that needed people.

The number is what you can put on a WhatsApp status. Strength is only one way to get the number.

If you handle money well, you have some left at the end of the month. You have some left, so you handle money well.

You may have moved back home. You may have got a bonus. You may have skipped a bill you still owe.

Money left over is the result. Skill is one cause.

The same mistake runs on holiday photos. The trip is up, so the decisions must have been good.

If a business is failing, the stock price falls. The price fell, so the business is failing.

Prices fall because rates moved, because the whole sector had a bad week, because one headline was ugly, because a large holder sold.

A falling price is not a report on the health of the company. Engineers who trade still talk as if it is. So do managers at lunch.

If a scheme is a scam, the promised return looks absurd. The return looks absurd, so it is a scam.

Sometimes that number really is a scam. Sometimes a young business, or a number quoted without the risk beside it, also looks absurd.

"It looks too good" is a different sentence from "this is a fraud".

You still have to ask what else produces a wild number. And you still should not give your savings to a stranger because the proof has a hole in it.

A hole in the proof is not safety.

If they were careless with money, the account would be empty. The account is empty, so they were careless.

Empty also comes from a hospital bill, a late salary, money sent home, a rent that jumped. Relatives are very sure about this one.

Being sure is not the same as having the cause.

Careers and exams

If someone is good at the job, they get promoted. They got promoted, so they are good.

Promotions follow timing, a boss who likes you, a seat that opened, a team that happened to do well, and sometimes actual skill.

The new title tells you who received the title.

If you work hard, you stay late. They stay late, so they work hard.

Staying late is also slow work, bad planning, a long commute, or a person who wants the chair to look full. A lot of offices treat the occupied chair as the work.

Managers with MBAs say this with a straight face. So do people who never sat in a management class.

The degree does not stop the sentence.

If a candidate is strong, they went to a well known college. They have a good JEE Advanced rank, so they are strong.

The rank can be one exam on one day, two years of coaching, a city, and money at home. It can also be years of actually being very good. The rank certificate does not say which.

Hiring still uses the reverse. It is fast. And everyone else in the room is nodding.

And trust me, a lot of the people nodding already know better. They do it anyway.

If the code is wrong, the tests fail. The tests failed, so the code is wrong.

The test can be wrong. The data can be wrong. The machine you ran it on can be wrong.

Red on a screen feels like engineering because a program printed it.

It is still the reversed sentence.

If a student is serious, the marks are high. The board marks are high, so the student is serious.

An easy paper can do that. So can a lucky set. So can a tuition that drilled that exact paper.

Teachers do this. Students do it about each other. Parents do it the most.

If a founder is serious, they have raised money. They raised money, so they are serious.

Money also shows up for a clean story, a known college, a hot market, a friend of an investor. A transfer into the account is not a test of character.

News

If a story matters, it leads the page. It leads the page, so it matters.

The top of a page follows fear, clicks, and whatever happened in the last hour. A fire two kilometres away can sit above a slow problem that will hit many more people.

Journalists know this about their own desk. They still feel it when they are the ones scrolling.

If a source is lying, the account has holes. The account has holes, so the source is lying.

Holes come from memory. From fear. From a bad recording. From a person who was not looking at the thing you asked about.

A careful reporter treats a hole as a reason to ask again. The reversed sentence treats the hole as a verdict.

That verdict gets published by people whose job is to check things.

The job does not make the mistake impossible. It makes the mistake sound like a finding.

If a video is fake, it looks a bit off. It looks a bit off, so it is fake.

Real footage looks off all the time. Bad light. A cheap phone. Compression. A strange angle.

"It looks weird" is a different sentence from "someone made this up".

If a report is solid, a big paper ran it. A big paper ran it, so it is solid.

Big papers also run things that are thin, early, or copied from each other before the deadline. The logo is a decision made in a hurry.

It is not a second investigation that you watched.

Parenting

If a child is happy at school, they talk about it at dinner. They talked about school, so they are happy.

They may have talked because you asked. Because one funny thing happened in the break. Because they are a child who talks.

A story about lunch is not a report on the day.

If a parent is neglectful, the child acts out. The child is acting out, so the parent is neglectful.

Acting out also comes from a new sibling, a bully, hunger, a class they cannot follow, a week of bad sleep.

Relatives jump to the parent. So do teachers, including teachers with very strong degrees. That was the cause they already wanted.

The behaviour is what you saw. The home is one place to look.

If you are raising them well, they are polite to guests. They were polite, so you are raising them well.

One evening of hello uncle can be fear, a promised chocolate, or a child who is fine with guests and miserable at home. The guests still announce the result in the car.

They saw B. They declared A.

If the child studied, the marks came out well. The marks came out well, so the child studied.

The paper may have been kind. They may have guessed well. A coaching class may have drilled that paper.

The marks go on the fridge. Studying is the cause adults prefer, so they treat it as the only one.

Work

If someone has checked out, their camera is off. The camera is off, so they have checked out.

The camera is also off for a bad connection, a sleeping baby, a face they do not want recorded, or a person who is typing. A person can run a large team and still treat a black box as a reading of character.

If the meeting was useful, people spoke. People spoke, so the meeting was useful.

People speak to be seen. They speak to repeat a point. They speak because the silence felt long.

Noise has many causes. Use was one of them.

The status update has the same shape. If the project is on track, the update sounds confident. The update sounded confident, so the project is on track.

Confidence is a tone. A tone is cheap to produce.

If a teammate is sharp, they reply fast on the group. They replied fast, so they are sharp.

Fast can be the person who was already at the laptop. The slow reply may be the one who went and checked.

If they respected you, they would agree in the meeting. They agreed, so they respect you.

Agreement is also fear, tiredness, or a wish to leave the room. The nod is what you saw. Respect is the flattering cause. That is why it sticks.

Politics

I am not taking a side in this section. The mistake works for every side. It does not need a party name.

If a policy worked, some number moved the way people hoped. The number moved, so the policy worked.

Numbers move because of weather, a festival, a different rule, a change in how the count is done, and people changing their habits for reasons that had nothing to do with the speech.

Whoever is in office points at the number. Whoever is out of office points at a different number and does the same thing.

A degree in economics does not stop you when the number flatters you.

If a politician is corrupt, they own expensive things. They own expensive things, so they are corrupt.

Expensive things also come from a family, a spouse's job, a loan, a business that was already there before the election. A watch you can see is not a charge sheet.

A cheap watch is not proof of honesty either. People use that cousin of the mistake when they already like the person.

If voters wanted that law, they voted for the people who proposed it. They voted for those people, so they wanted that law.

People vote for a local face, a habit, a community, a job, a fear, one other issue that mattered more that year. Reading the win as a signature on every line of the list is the reverse sentence.

It does not become true because the person saying it has read a lot of politics.

Why trained people still do it

The two sentences use the same words.

Rain, then wet ground. Wet ground, then rain.

If the first one sounded sensible, the ear lets the second one through. It feels like repetition.

It is not repetition. You turned the arrow around.

It is also fast. Asking what else could cause B takes a list. B happened, so A, is already done.

You get a verdict without waiting. A verdict feels like thought.

And it protects a story you already liked.

The quiet partner is cheating. The promoted colleague is brilliant. The polite child proves the parenting. The number on the screen proves your side.

School gives you more words for the story. It does not force you to list the other causes.

Think of a court, because that is where people are most sure they are being logical.

If the person did it, they will look nervous. They look nervous, so they did it.

A courtroom is a frightening room. Innocent people sweat. Guilty people sweat. A person who slept badly and then got accused in public also sweats.

Nervousness is what you can see. Guilt is one cause of it.

That is the harsh reality of this habit. A degree does not remove it. It only gives the habit a cleaner vocabulary.

The sooner you realize it the better it is. The next conversation you are in will contain the reversed sentence.

What to do with it

When the conclusion shows up as "B, therefore A", say both sentences out loud.

Rain makes the ground wet.

Wet ground means it rained.

The second one has to stand by itself. A lot of the time it cannot. That is useful. Very often the second one is the sentence people are actually using.

Then ask what else can produce B. Two other causes are enough to stop the jump. If those causes are still possible, you do not have A.

You have B, and a guess.

The key thing to take away from this chapter is as follows:

A implies B is one sentence. B implies A is another sentence. Using the same words does not make them the same fact. The result in front of you is not proof of the cause you already prefer. Say both directions out loud. If the reverse sounds weaker, it is weaker. Do not spend your money, your reading of another person, or your reading of the news on a sentence that was only ever true the other way.

Technical Viewpoint

If you want the same point in symbols, here it is. The life above does not depend on this part. This part only names the split.

A implies B is written \( A \Rightarrow B \). The converse is \( B \Rightarrow A \). They are different statements.

The truth table makes the split obvious. Look at the row where A is false and B is true. The implication holds. The converse does not.

That row is wet ground, and no rain.

$$ \begin{array}{c|c|c|c} A & B & A \Rightarrow B & B \Rightarrow A \\ \hline \mathrm{T} & \mathrm{T} & \mathrm{T} & \mathrm{T} \\ \mathrm{T} & \mathrm{F} & \mathrm{F} & \mathrm{T} \\ \mathrm{F} & \mathrm{T} & \mathrm{T} & \mathrm{F} \\ \mathrm{F} & \mathrm{F} & \mathrm{T} & \mathrm{T} \end{array} $$

Probability has the same split, with two different numbers. \( P(B \mid A) \) is the chance of B when you already know A. \( P(A \mid B) \) is the chance of A when you already know B.

People hear a high \( P(B \mid A) \) and walk around as if they were told \( P(A \mid B) \).

Bayes' rule is the link between them.

$$ P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(B \mid A)\, P(A)}{P(B)} $$

If A really does imply B, then \( P(B \mid A) = 1 \), and the formula becomes this.

$$ P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(A)}{P(B)} $$

That fraction is 1 only when B does not show up without A. In ordinary life B has other causes, so \( P(B) \) is larger than \( P(A) \), and \( P(A \mid B) \) is smaller than 1.

The implication can be solid. The reverse can still be a bad bet.

That is all the symbols are saying.
